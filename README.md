### About me

I'm a developer based in Okinawa, Japan.

What I actually do is build web apps and then run them myself. Not on a
managed platform — on a couple of small cloud servers I set up and keep alive
by hand. So I end up touching all of it: the backend, the database, the nginx
config, the TLS certificates, and whatever decides to break at a bad hour. I'd
rather understand the whole thing than rent most of it and hope.

Most of what I've made lives at **[bikrant666.com.np](https://bikrant666.com.np)**.
I write there too, mostly guides for problems I had to work out the hard way,
in English and Japanese.
